AEM Guides supports operations across folders and selections. Versioning, metadata edits, and more.

## Time-savers
- Apply metadata to a whole folder.
- Version or label many topics together.
- Move or reorganize in bulk.

## Takeaway
When you catch yourself repeating an action, look for the bulk or folder-level equivalent first.
