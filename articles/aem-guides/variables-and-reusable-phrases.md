Reusable phrases (via conref or keyref) let you define text once. Like a product name. And reuse it everywhere.

## Benefits
- Rebrand or rename in a single edit.
- Guarantee consistent terminology.
- Reduce review effort.

## Takeaway
Any phrase you repeat is a candidate for reuse. Define it once and let references do the rest.
