DITA (Darwin Information Typing Architecture) is the XML standard behind AEM Guides. You don't need to be an XML expert, but a few ideas help.

## Core concepts
- **Topics** are small, standalone units of content.
- **Maps** assemble topics into a deliverable and define order and hierarchy.
- **Topic types** (concept, task, reference) give content predictable structure.
- **Reuse** is built in through references, not copy-paste.

## Takeaway
Think in small, typed, reusable topics. That mindset is what makes DITA. And AEM Guides. Powerful.
