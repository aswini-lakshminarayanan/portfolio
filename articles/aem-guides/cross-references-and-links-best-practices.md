Prefer structured links. **xref**, **keyref**, and **reltables**. Over raw URLs inside topics.

## Guidelines
- Use keyref for links that may change or vary by output.
- Use reltables for related-topic links.
- Reserve direct xref for tightly coupled content.

## Takeaway
Structured linking survives reorganization and reuse. Avoid hard-coded paths wherever you can.
