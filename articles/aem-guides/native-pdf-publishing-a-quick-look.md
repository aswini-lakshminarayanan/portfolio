**Native PDF** publishing in AEM Guides uses Adobe's own engine, offering a modern, template-driven PDF workflow.

## Highlights
- Rich, CSS-like styling control.
- Faster iteration on design.
- Strong fidelity for complex layouts.

## Takeaway
If PDF design matters to you, evaluate native PDF. It gives designers far more control.
