Reviewers can highlight text and leave threaded comments directly on the content.

## Tips
- Keep comments specific and actionable.
- Use threads to resolve discussion in place.
- Track status so nothing slips through.

## Takeaway
Treat review comments as a to-do list. Clear, resolved threads mean faster, cleaner sign-off.
