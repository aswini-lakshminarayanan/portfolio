**Chunking** controls how topics combine into output pages. Get it right and reuse becomes effortless.

## Principles
- One topic = one idea.
- Avoid topics too large to reuse or too small to stand alone.
- Use chunking attributes to control page generation.

## Takeaway
Well-chunked topics are the unit of reuse. Size them for reuse first, output second.
