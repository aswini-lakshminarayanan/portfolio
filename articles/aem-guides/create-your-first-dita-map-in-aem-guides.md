A **map** is the backbone of any deliverable in AEM Guides. It lists the topics to include and the order they appear in.

## Steps
1. In the Repository view, choose a folder and create a new **DITA Map**.
2. Open it in the **Map Editor**.
3. Add topic references (topicrefs) by dragging topics or creating new ones.
4. Arrange them to set your table of contents.

## Takeaway
Start every project with a map. It becomes your outline, your TOC, and your publishing unit all at once.
