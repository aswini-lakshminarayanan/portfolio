Every save can create a version in AEM Guides, giving you a full history of each topic and map.

## What you can do
- Compare versions to see what changed.
- Revert to an earlier version.
- Label versions for milestones.

## Takeaway
Versioning is your safety net. Edit freely, knowing you can always look back or roll back.
