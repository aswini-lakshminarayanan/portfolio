PDF templates control the look of your published documents. Covers, page layout, headers, footers, and typography.

## What you can customize
- Cover pages and branding.
- Page headers/footers and numbering.
- Fonts, colors, and spacing.

## Takeaway
Invest once in a strong PDF template and every future publish looks on-brand automatically.
