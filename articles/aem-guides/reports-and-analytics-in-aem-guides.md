AEM Guides offers reports on content status, reuse, links, and more.

## Useful reports
- **Reuse reports** to see where topics are used.
- **Link reports** to find broken references.
- **Status reports** for release readiness.

## Takeaway
Reports turn a large repository into something you can actually measure and manage.
