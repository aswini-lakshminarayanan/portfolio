AEM Guides supports **search and replace** across many topics at once. A lifesaver for renames and corrections.

## Use it for
- Product or feature renames.
- Fixing a recurring typo or term.
- Locating every use of a phrase before editing.

## Takeaway
Repository-wide search and replace turns hours of manual edits into a single, reviewable operation.
