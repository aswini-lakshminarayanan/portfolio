Starting a translation project in AEM Guides means selecting the source, the targets, and the workflow.

## Options
- **Human translation** via connected vendors.
- **Machine translation** for speed or drafts.
- Mixed approaches per language.

## Takeaway
Decide human vs machine per audience and content type. High-stakes content deserves human review.
