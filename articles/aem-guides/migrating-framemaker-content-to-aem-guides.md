AEM Guides supports importing FrameMaker content, converting it toward clean DITA.

## Plan for
- **Mapping** styles to DITA elements.
- **Cleanup** of legacy formatting.
- **Restructuring** long documents into topics.

## Takeaway
Migration is as much content strategy as tooling. Budget time to topic-ize and clean up, not just import.
