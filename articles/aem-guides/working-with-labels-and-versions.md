**Labels** are human-friendly names you attach to specific versions. Like "v2.0 release" or "approved".

## Why label
- Quickly locate a milestone version.
- Build baselines from labeled content.
- Communicate status across the team.

## Takeaway
Labels turn a long version history into a set of meaningful, findable checkpoints.
