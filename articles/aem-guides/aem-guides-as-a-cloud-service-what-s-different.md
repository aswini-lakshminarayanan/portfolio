AEM Guides is available on **AEM as a Cloud Service**, which changes how you deploy, scale, and update.

## What to know
- Adobe manages infrastructure and updates.
- Continuous delivery instead of big upgrades.
- Cloud-native scaling for publishing.

## Takeaway
On Cloud Service you get always-current AEM Guides and less ops overhead. Plan your workflows around continuous updates.
