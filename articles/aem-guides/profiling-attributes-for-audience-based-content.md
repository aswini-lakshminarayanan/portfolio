Profiling attributes (like `audience`, `platform`, `product`) mark content for specific readers. Combined with filtering, they drive tailored outputs.

## Setup tips
- Define your profiling values centrally so authors pick from a list.
- Apply attributes at the smallest sensible level (element, not whole topic).
- Test each profile before release.

## Takeaway
Well-planned profiling keeps a single source serving many audiences without forks.
