A good map structure keeps large projects navigable and reusable.

## Guidelines
- Use **submaps** to break big deliverables into parts.
- Keep hierarchy shallow and logical.
- Separate reusable submaps from deliverable-specific ones.

## Takeaway
Design your maps like software modules: small, composable, and reusable across deliverables.
