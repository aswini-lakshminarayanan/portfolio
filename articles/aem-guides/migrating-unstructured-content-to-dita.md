Moving unstructured content (Word, HTML) into DITA is mostly about **topic-based thinking**.

## Steps
1. Break documents into standalone topics.
2. Assign each a topic type.
3. Identify reuse opportunities.
4. Import and validate.

## Takeaway
Don't just lift and shift. Restructure into topics first. That's where the long-term value is.
