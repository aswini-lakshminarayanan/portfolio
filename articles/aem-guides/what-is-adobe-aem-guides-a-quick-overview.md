Adobe Experience Manager Guides (AEM Guides) is a native, DITA-based **Component Content Management System (CCMS)** built into AEM. It lets teams author, manage, and publish structured technical content at scale.

## Why teams use it
- **Structured authoring** with DITA topics and maps.
- **Reuse** of content across deliverables, so you write once and publish everywhere.
- **Multi-channel publishing** to PDF, responsive HTML5, and more.
- **Governance** through versioning, reviews, and translation workflows.

## Takeaway
If you manage large, frequently updated documentation, AEM Guides brings structure, reuse, and automated publishing into one place.
