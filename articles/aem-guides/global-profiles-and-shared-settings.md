Global profiles and settings centralize choices like profiling values, metadata schemas, and editor behavior.

## Benefits
- Consistent options for all authors.
- Fewer configuration mistakes.
- Easier onboarding.

## Takeaway
Configure shared settings early. Consistency at the platform level prevents chaos at the content level.
