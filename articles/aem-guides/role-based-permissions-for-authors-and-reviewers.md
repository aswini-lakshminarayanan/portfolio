AEM's permission model lets you scope who can author, review, publish, or administer.

## Typical roles
- **Authors** create and edit topics.
- **Reviewers** comment but don't edit.
- **Admins** manage settings and presets.

## Takeaway
Match permissions to responsibilities. Clear roles keep content safe and workflows clean.
