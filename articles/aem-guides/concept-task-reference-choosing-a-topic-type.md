DITA's three main topic types keep content consistent and reusable.

## The three types
- **Concept**. Explains what something is or why it matters.
- **Task**. Step-by-step instructions to accomplish a goal.
- **Reference**. Lookup information like tables, parameters, or specs.

## Rule of thumb
Ask what the reader is doing: understanding (concept), doing (task), or looking up (reference).

## Takeaway
Matching content to the right topic type makes it predictable to write, reuse, and read.
