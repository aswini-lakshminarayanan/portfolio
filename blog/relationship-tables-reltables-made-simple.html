Relationship tables (reltables) made simple

AEM GuidesDITA

A relationship table (reltable) in a map defines links between topics. For example, linking each task to its related concept and reference.

Why use one

  • Keep topics free of hard-coded related links.
  • Manage relationships in one place.
  • Generate consistent "related links" sections automatically.

Takeaway

Reltables keep your topics portable and your cross-links consistent across the whole deliverable.
