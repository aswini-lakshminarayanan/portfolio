Managing metadata in AEM Guides

AEM GuidesGovernance

Metadata describes your content. Audience, product, status, and more. AEM Guides lets you apply it at the topic and map level.

What to capture

  • Audience and product for filtering.
  • Status (draft, in review, approved).
  • Custom properties for your governance needs.

Takeaway

Consistent metadata pays off later: better search, cleaner reports, and reliable conditional publishing.
