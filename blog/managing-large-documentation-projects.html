Managing large documentation projects

AEM GuidesStrategy

Scale changes everything. Large projects need structure, standards, and automation.

What helps

  • A clear folder and naming taxonomy.
  • Shared metadata and profiling standards.
  • Reusable submaps and presets.
  • Regular reports and link checks.

Takeaway

At scale, conventions beat heroics. Invest in standards early and they pay off every day.
