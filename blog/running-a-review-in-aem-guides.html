Running a review in AEM Guides

AEM GuidesReviews

The built-in review workflow lets subject-matter experts comment on content in context.

Flow

  1. Author starts a review and adds reviewers.
  2. Reviewers annotate and comment in the browser.
  3. The author addresses feedback and closes the review.

Takeaway

In-context reviews replace scattered email and PDF markups with a single, trackable conversation.
