Reusing content with content references (conref)

AEM GuidesReuse

A content reference (conref) pulls an element from one topic into another. Update the source, and every place that references it updates too.

Good candidates for conref

  • Warnings and safety notices.
  • Product names and boilerplate.
  • Shared steps or notes.

How it works

Give the source element an id, then reference it from other topics.

Takeaway

Conref eliminates copy-paste drift. Maintain one source of truth and reuse it everywhere.
