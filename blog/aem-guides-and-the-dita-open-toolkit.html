AEM Guides and the DITA Open Toolkit

AEM GuidesPublishing

Under the hood, AEM Guides uses the DITA Open Toolkit (DITA-OT) for many outputs, extended with Adobe's publishing engine.

Why it matters

  • Standard DITA-OT plugins can be leveraged.
  • Native PDF publishing offers a modern alternative for PDF.
  • Customization is possible without leaving standards.

Takeaway

You get open-standard publishing plus Adobe's enhancements. Flexibility without lock-in.
