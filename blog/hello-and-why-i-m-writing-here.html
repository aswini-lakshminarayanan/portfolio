Hello, and why I'm writing here

IntroCareer

For ten years I've worked inside Adobe Experience Manager projects: as a developer, a technical lead, a business analyst, and now as a Solutions Consultant at Adobe. Most of what I learned lived in project notes, workshop whiteboards, and conversations that ended when the engagement did.

This is my attempt to change that: a place to write down the patterns, mistakes, and small wins that actually move enterprise projects forward.

What I'll write about

  • AEM in practice: architecture, headless, components, and the decisions that age well (and the ones that don't).
  • Solution consulting: how to turn a fuzzy business goal into a design a C-level audience will trust and a team can build.
  • Delivery: requirements, enablement, and the unglamorous habits that keep enterprise programs on track.

Each post also goes out on LinkedIn, but this is the home version, a little longer, with the reasoning left in.

The best solution architecture is the one your stakeholders understand well enough to defend when you're not in the room.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
