Baseline management for consistent releases

AEM GuidesGovernance

A baseline captures specific versions of every topic in a map. Publish from the baseline and you get exactly that snapshot. No surprises from in-progress edits.

When to use one

  • Locking a release while work continues on the next version.
  • Reproducing an older published output.

Takeaway

Use baselines whenever "what we shipped" must stay stable and reproducible.
