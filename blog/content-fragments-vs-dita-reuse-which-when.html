Content fragments vs DITA reuse: which when?

AEM GuidesStrategy

AEM Sites offers content fragments; AEM Guides offers DITA reuse. They solve different problems.

Rough guide

  • DITA reuse for structured, versioned technical content and multi-format publishing.
  • Content fragments for channel-agnostic marketing content delivered via APIs.

Takeaway

Pick DITA reuse for documentation depth; pick content fragments for headless delivery. They can coexist.
