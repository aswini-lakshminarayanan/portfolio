Welcome — why I started writing here

MetaWriting

I've spent years solving hard problems and taking notes only I would ever read. This is my attempt to change that — a home for the ideas, lessons, and occasional strong opinions I'd otherwise keep to myself.

What you'll find here

  • Field notes from the work I do day to day.
  • Deep dives on the tools and patterns I keep coming back to.
  • Short essays on how teams build good products.

Each post here also goes out on LinkedIn, but this is the canonical version — longer, with the rough edges left in.

How to follow along

The fastest way is to connect with me on LinkedIn. New writing shows up there and on the home page.

Writing forces you to find out what you actually think. That's the whole point.
